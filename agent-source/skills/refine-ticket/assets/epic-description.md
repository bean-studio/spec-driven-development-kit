## Problem

What is wrong or missing across the whole epic, and the evidence for it:
observed versus expected, and where it is observable. No solution, no design.

## Boundary

The outer edge of the epic as prose, not a work breakdown. State each exclusion
inline as "Not in scope: ...". Child items carry the numbered, separately
verifiable pieces of work; do not restate them here.

## Decomposition

One line per child item: its tracker key and the one-line scope it owns. Every
line names an item that exists in the tracker and is linked to this epic. The
lines together cover the boundary and nothing outside it. Sequencing and
dependencies use the tracker's native links, not prose here.

## Open questions

One per line, each marked blocking or non-blocking, and only questions that
belong to the epic rather than to a single child. Refinement removes a question
once its answer is recorded in the Decomposition Ready record. Remove the
section when empty.

## Exit criteria

One checkable statement per line: what must be observably true once every child
item is done. These are epic-level outcomes, never a restatement of a child's
acceptance criteria.
